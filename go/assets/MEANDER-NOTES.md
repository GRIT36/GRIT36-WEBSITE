# Meander question bank and photographs

Source: user-provided Aardrijkskunde_Oefentoets_Groep6.docx. This is a practice bank, not an official school examination or official Meander question set.

The 36 numbered source items consist of 18 single-choice questions, 12 multiple-choice questions and 6 matching pairs. All source item numbers are retained in meander-felix.json. The six pairs are grouped into two questions of three pairs (landscape and topography). Each round has 5 single-choice questions, 3 multiple-choice questions and these 2 matching questions, with shuffled options/pair positions. Each question is worth one point; multiple-choice selections and all pairs must be completely correct.

Edits made during review:
- Item 1: limited the general statement to hills and sandy areas affected by ice ages.
- Item 3: clarified that wind deposited much of the cover sand during the last ice age; the answer remains B.
- Item 9: compared ice-pushed ridges and marine-clay areas, rather than mixing a landform with a soil term.
- Item 17: used the full river name Overijsselse Vecht and clarified its course toward the Zwarte Water near Zwolle.
- Items 19, 20 and 24: avoided saying every part of high/low Netherlands was formed only by ice/sea.
- Matching explanations: clarified that an ice-pushed ridge is pushed up by ice, peat is accumulated plant matter in wet conditions, and marine clay is deposited by seawater.

Topographic content must still be checked against the teacher's actual classroom map. This practice set does not replace locating places on that map.

Photo search keywords, creators, source pages and individual licenses are in meander-photos.json and displayed below each photo. Photos are locally bundled Wikimedia Commons thumbnails; only display scaling/cropping is used. No generative images or unrelated random fallback photographs are used. The photograph licenses apply independently from the application code. If a local photo fails to load, the question remains answerable and the source credit remains visible.

History uses the existing learning_sessions table with mode mc and list_id geo:Felix:meander-landschap. It stores the selected learner, duration, counts and incorrect-answer explanations. Geography records are excluded from word-accuracy trends and word-difficulty ranking. No additional database migration is needed beyond the existing history setup.
