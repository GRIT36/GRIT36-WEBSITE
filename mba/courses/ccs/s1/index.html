The reading path

7 frameworks & concepts

S01 / Foundations

The strategic management process

Strategy links long-term direction, choices and resource allocation. The course treats it as a connected process: strategic inputs inform formulation and implementation; outcomes and experience feed back into the next round of decisions.

Read the framework ↗S01 / Foundations

Industry position & resource-based advantage

The I/O model and the resource-based model explain above-average returns from different starting points. One begins with industry structure; the other begins with the firm's resources and capabilities. They provide complementary questions for diagnosing an opportunity.

Read the framework ↗S01 / Strategy formation

Six schools of strategy

The course distinguishes prescriptive schools, which explain how strategy should be formulated, from descriptive schools, which examine how strategy actually forms. Each emphasizes different actors, assumptions and sources of insight.

Read the framework ↗S01 / Strategy formation

Deliberate & emergent strategy

Realized strategy can contain both deliberate choices and patterns that emerge through action. The lecture separates what was intended from what was implemented, abandoned or learned along the way.

Read the framework ↗S01 / Industry analysis

Porter's Five Forces

Five Forces examines how competition over economic value extends beyond direct rivals. The focal unit is an industry: suppliers, buyers, substitutes and potential entrants can all constrain its profit potential.

Read the framework ↗S01 / Positioning

Generic competitive strategies

Generic strategies combine the source of advantage with the breadth of the competitive target. The central choices are cost leadership, differentiation and focus; the slides also discuss integrated or hybrid positioning.

Read the framework ↗S01 / Positioning

Bowman's Strategy Clock

The Strategy Clock maps competitive positions using price and perceived value to the customer. It makes room for combinations such as low price with low added value, low price, hybrid value, differentiation and focused differentiation.
