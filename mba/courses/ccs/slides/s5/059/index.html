Choice of alliance partner
 Managing risks and uncertainties (sharing risks)
 Overcoming funding limitations
 Improving economies of scope or scale (lower average-production costs)
 Access to complementary resources such as new technologies
 Access to new markets (e.g., making use of the partner’s market reputation, knowledge of local/regional market, political 
contracts, distributional channels, etc.)
 Strengthening one’s position in the marketplace: leveraging in-house R&D capabilities, ensuring a more reliable supply 
chain, negotiating purchasing discounts, creating a more encompassing value proposition, acquire data, etc.)
 Speeding things up (e.g., new technology or product development, market entry, etc.)
 Other potential reasons: allowing for greater flexibility, enhanced learning, closing of certain skill gaps, generation of 
additional income streams, and more lobbying power.
