Introduction to the concept of corporate scope
 The multi-business company can be conceived of as an assembly of (groups of) business units, each 
of which is responsible for the company’s competitive posture in a distinctive business area.
 The nature of a company’s business areas can be described in terms of either the industries(industry-
oriented approach) or markets (market-oriented approach) in which it has a competitive presence.
 An industry is a group of firms producing similar products and competing for 
the same resources and customers in the marketplace.
 A product market consists of products (goods or services) with similar 
characteristics in terms of features, performance, intended use, and price.
 A geographical market is a distinct geographical area in which products are 
sold and in which the conditions of competition are relatively homogeneous.
