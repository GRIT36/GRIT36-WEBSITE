Session 2 / Business model

Four digital business model types

The course's digital business model matrix combines two dimensions: knowledge of the end customer, from partial to complete, and business design, from a value chain to an ecosystem. The resulting four positions imply different relationships and capabilities.

ENGLISH STUDY GUIDE Edited explanation based on the course. Original wording and figures appear below.

The framework

ModelPosition in the matrixCharacteristics
SupplierValue chain / partial customer knowledgeSells through another company; can rely on low-cost production and incremental innovation, but may have limited direct customer access.
Omnichannel businessValue chain / complete customer knowledgeCoordinates an integrated value chain and a multiproduct, multichannel customer relationship.
Modular producerEcosystem / partial customer knowledgeOffers a plug-and-play product or service that can adapt to different ecosystems; relies on continued product or service innovation.
Ecosystem driverEcosystem / complete customer knowledgeProvides a platform and customer experience, connects third-party offers to customer needs and uses information across interactions.

Key distinctions

What changes between positions?

The distinction is not simply whether a company has a website. It concerns access to the end customer, the scope of the experience it coordinates and how its offer connects with other participants. A shift toward an ecosystem can change both dependencies and the ability to capture value.

Use the axes explicitly

Identify where customer knowledge comes from and which activities the firm coordinates. A company may combine activities associated with several positions, so specify the business being analyzed rather than forcing the whole company into one label.

How to use it

  1. Assess the depth of the direct end-customer relationship.
  2. Map whether delivery follows a controlled chain or a broader ecosystem.
  3. Identify the capabilities and dependencies implied by the chosen position.

Keep in mind

The matrix is a simplification. A more expansive role does not automatically create more value or justify the resources needed.

Pause & recall

How does a modular producer differ from an ecosystem driver?

Reveal the explanation

The modular producer supplies an adaptable component; the driver coordinates a broader platform and customer relationship.

Replication, renewal & dual focus

Business model replication develops and extends an existing logic; business model renewal changes the model more substantially. The lecture distinguishes fixation, a focus on one route and dual focus on both routes.

Business model transformation routes

The innovation matrix combines replication versus renewal with strategy-driven versus customer-driven change. Its four routes link the direction of change to leadership, managerial involvement, knowledge absorption and organizational identity.
