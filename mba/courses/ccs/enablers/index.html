Session 2 / Business model innovation

Enablers & inhibitors of innovation

The same transformation proposal can progress differently in different organizations. The course groups enabling and inhibiting conditions around culture, management, institutions, external orientation and organizational characteristics.

Key distinctions

The existing-customer tension

Customers can provide ideas, but an exclusive focus on current customers may inhibit renewal toward new customers. The lecture presents this as a tension between improving the existing offer and discovering needs beyond it, not as a reason to ignore customer evidence.

Translate diagnosis into action

The teaching sequence asks both how to strengthen enablers and how to neutralize inhibitors. A factor only becomes useful in a strategy review when it is connected to a specific mechanism and an action that someone can take.

How to use it

  1. Name the preferred transformation route.
  2. Identify the two or three conditions most likely to accelerate or obstruct it.
  3. Specify an action, responsible person and observable signal for each condition.

Keep in mind

Associations shown in teaching material should not be treated as deterministic laws. For example, a reported tenure pattern does not establish a universal ideal tenure for every CEO.

Pause & recall

When can customer orientation inhibit renewal?

Reveal the explanation

When attention to present customers prevents the firm from examining different needs or future customer groups.

Four digital business model types

The course's digital business model matrix combines two dimensions: knowledge of the end customer, from partial to complete, and business design, from a value chain to an ecosystem. The resulting four positions imply different relationships and capabilities.

Replication, renewal & dual focus

Business model replication develops and extends an existing logic; business model renewal changes the model more substantially. The lecture distinguishes fixation, a focus on one route and dual focus on both routes.

Business model transformation routes

The innovation matrix combines replication versus renewal with strategy-driven versus customer-driven change. Its four routes link the direction of change to leadership, managerial involvement, knowledge absorption and organizational identity.

Four levers of business model innovation

Technology can enable new activities, but it is only one of the course's four levers. Management practices, organizational forms and co-creation affect whether an innovation can actually change how value is created and captured.
