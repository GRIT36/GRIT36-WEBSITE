03 / ABOUT GRIT36

Determination
meets strategy.

GRIT36 is a strategic consulting business based in Uithoorn, the Netherlands, working with startups and SMEs.

Our focus spans international trade, retail and supply chains, supported by competitive analysis, business data and negotiation. These are connected parts of a business—not isolated disciplines.

The ambition is simple: bring clarity to your business decisions and a practical direction to what comes next.

OUR PERSPECTIVE / STRATEGY IN THE AGE OF AI

More possibilities.
More need for judgment.

AI makes it easier to turn ideas into action. When so much can be built, tested or launched, the harder question is no longer just “Can we do this?” but “Should we?”

We believe the value of strategy lies increasingly in that judgment: deciding what deserves attention, what to leave aside and which opportunities do not serve your goals. The ability to do more does not make everything worth doing.
